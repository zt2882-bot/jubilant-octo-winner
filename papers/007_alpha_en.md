Abstract
 
Aiming at the first cause dilemma faced by traditional cosmology, the probability paradox of the anthropic principle, and the measure conundrum of the quantum many-worlds interpretation, this paper constructs a hierarchical cosmic probability model based on standard measure theory and Kolmogorov's axiomatic probability theory. The core proposition of the model is: the global outer measure of the total-state universe space containing all possibilities is strictly zero; while for every local probability bubble satisfying causal closure and rule self-consistency, its intrinsic probability measure must be normalized to 1. Without introducing additional physical hypotheses, the model only through the hierarchical division of measures and perspective transformation, realizes a self-consistent explanation of "creation ex nihilo" of the origin of the universe, and resolves the classic intuitive contradiction between "zero probability and possible occurrence". It provides a unified mathematical framework for the core problems of cosmology, quantum interpretation, and probability philosophy.
 
Keywords
 
Lebesgue null set; probability measure; self-consistent probability bubble; cosmological model; axiomatic probability theory; many-worlds interpretation
 
1. Introduction
 
The development of modern cosmology and quantum mechanics has been accompanied by a series of core dilemmas in underlying logic and probability philosophy:
 
1. The first cause problem of cosmic origin: Both the traditional Big Bang model and the cosmic inflation theory cannot avoid the dilemma of "where the initial conditions of the universe come from". Any model that presupposes a positive-probability initial state cannot get rid of the dependence on the "first cause".
2. The probability paradox of the anthropic principle: The fundamental physical constants and cosmological parameters of the observable universe satisfy the habitable conditions with an extremely low probability. Under the traditional probability framework, the conclusion that "a small-probability event happens to occur" has always faced logical far-fetchedness.
3. The measure conundrum of the quantum many-worlds interpretation: In Everett's many-worlds interpretation, the definition and normalization of probability weights for countless world branches generated by quantum decoherence have always been controversial, and it is impossible to explain the reality that "why observers can only perceive a specific branch".
4. The fundamental contradiction of probability philosophy: The intuition that "zero probability is equivalent to impossible event" in discrete probability scenarios is completely broken in continuous probability space, but the underlying logic of "why a zero-probability event can occur" has never been incorporated into a unified framework of cosmology.
 
The core commonality of the above dilemmas is that traditional models always default to the "normalization of global probability", that is, the possibility space of the entire universe must satisfy the constraint of total probability of 1, and confuse the hierarchical boundary between "global measure" and "local measure".
 
This paper jumps out of this default assumption, and proposes a "global zero measure - local normalization" self-consistent probability bubble universe model based on ZFC axiomatic set theory, Lebesgue measure theory and Kolmogorov's axiomatic probability theory. The model divides the "total-state universe" and "locally self-consistent universe" into two completely independent measure levels. Through strict mathematical definitions and theorem proofs, it realizes the self-consistent landing of the core propositions, and further explains the model's ability to resolve classic paradoxes and its physical and philosophical connotations.
 
2. Core Definitions and Axiomatic Foundations
 
All derivations in this paper are based on the standard mathematical axiom system, without introducing any additional assumptions beyond ZFC set theory, measure theory and axiomatic probability theory. This section first gives strict mathematical definitions of the core concepts of the model, and clarifies the boundaries of different measure levels.
 
2.1 Definition of Total-State Universe Space
 
Definition 1 (Total-State Universe Space): Denoted as \mathcal{U}, it is the complete set containing all conceivable cosmic states, whose elements cover:
 
1. All cosmic states satisfying causal closure, rule self-consistency, and definable evolution (i.e., elements of the "self-consistent probability bubble" defined later);
2. All non-causal chaotic states that do not satisfy self-consistency, have no stable rules, and have logical paradoxes.
 
\mathcal{U} is an infinite-dimensional topological measurable space, without preset physical rules, spacetime dimensions, or causal constraints, and is the largest set of all possibilities.
 
2.2 Definition of Global Outer Measure
 
Definition 2 (Global Outer Measure): Denoted as P_{total}, it is a general measure (non-probability measure) defined on the power set of the total-state universe space \mathcal{U}, satisfying the basic axioms of measure theory:
 
1. Non-negativity: For any measurable subset A \subseteq \mathcal{U}, P_{total}(A) \geq 0;
2. Null measure of empty set: P_{total}(\emptyset) = 0;
3. Countable additivity: For any countable family of pairwise disjoint measurable subsets \{A_i\}_{i=1}^\infty, P_{total}(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty A_i) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty P_{total}(A_i).
 
The core attribute of the global outer measure is that it is a measure defined from the "God's eye view" outside the total-state universe, not constrained by the "total measure of 1" in probability axioms. Its physical meaning is the measurement of the "global weight" of different state sets in the total-state space.
 
2.3 Strict Definition of Self-Consistent Probability Bubble
 
Definition 3 (Self-Consistent Probability Bubble): A measurable subset \mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{U} is called a self-consistent probability bubble if and only if it satisfies the following two core conditions:
 
1. Rule Self-Consistency and Causal Closure: All states, evolution paths, and observable events within \mathcal{B} follow a set of closed, non-contradictory, and reproducible intrinsic rules (such as physical laws, causality, logical rules). This set of rules can completely define the state evolution and event correlation within \mathcal{B} without logical paradoxes, and has no uncontrollable causal coupling with the states in \mathcal{U} \setminus \mathcal{B}.
2. Measurability and Support Completeness: A σ-algebra \mathcal{F}_\mathcal{B} (i.e., the set of all observable events within \mathcal{B}) can be defined on \mathcal{B}, and there exists a measure P_\mathcal{B} supported on \mathcal{B}, that is, for any A \cap \mathcal{B} = \emptyset, P_\mathcal{B}(A) = 0.
 
2.4 Definition of Intrinsic Probability Measure Within the Bubble
 
Definition 4 (Intrinsic Probability Measure Within the Bubble): For a self-consistent probability bubble \mathcal{B}, its intrinsic probability measure P_\mathcal{B} is a probability measure defined on the measurable space (\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{F}_\mathcal{B}), satisfying all axioms of Kolmogorov's axiomatic probability theory:
 
1. Non-negativity: For any event A \in \mathcal{F}_\mathcal{B}, P_\mathcal{B}(A) \geq 0;
2. Normalization: P_\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{B}) = 1;
3. Countable additivity: For any countable family of pairwise disjoint events \{A_i\}_{i=1}^\infty \subseteq \mathcal{F}_\mathcal{B}, P_\mathcal{B}(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty A_i) = \sum_{i=1}^\infty P_\mathcal{B}(A_i).
 
This measure is defined from the "observer's perspective" inside the bubble, and is only valid for events within the bubble. Its physical meaning is the relative occurrence probability of events inside the bubble.
 
3. Core Theorems and Rigorous Proofs
 
Based on the above definitions, this section carries out strict mathematical proofs of the two core propositions of the model, and clarifies the inevitability of "global zero measure" and "local normalization".
 
3.1 Global Zero Measure Theorem of the Total-State Universe Space
 
Theorem 1 (Global Zero Measure Theorem): For the total-state universe space \mathcal{U} and its global outer measure P_{total}, P_{total}(\mathcal{U}) = 0.
 
Proof: We complete the proof through three independent logical paths, all of which point to the inevitable conclusion of global zero measure.
 
Path 1: Proof of Null Measure under Self-Consistency Constraints
 
The total-state space \mathcal{U} can be divided into two disjoint subsets: the complete set of self-consistent bubbles \mathcal{S} = \bigcup_{\mathcal{B} \in \mathbb{B}} \mathcal{B} (where \mathbb{B} is the set of all self-consistent probability bubbles), and the set of non-self-consistent chaotic states \mathcal{C} = \mathcal{U} \setminus \mathcal{S}.
 
First, self-consistency is an extremely strong constraint on cosmic states. A set that can be called "self-consistent" must satisfy causal closure, non-contradictory rules, and definable evolution, which means its states must be limited by a set of finite or countably infinite constraints. In the infinite-dimensional total-state space \mathcal{U}, the subset satisfying finite/countably infinite constraints must be a null set in \mathcal{U}.
 
Analogous to the null measure of low-dimensional embedding: the n-dimensional Lebesgue measure of a k-dimensional submanifold (k < n) in n-dimensional Euclidean space is strictly zero. Similarly, all self-consistent bubbles \mathcal{B} are essentially low-dimensional embedded submanifolds in the infinite-dimensional total-state space \mathcal{U}, and their global outer measure satisfies P_{total}(\mathcal{B}) = 0.
 
Since the set \mathbb{B} of self-consistent bubbles is a countably infinite set (the set of definable self-consistent rules is countable), according to the countable additivity of measure:
P_{total}(\mathcal{S}) = P_{total}(\bigcup_{\mathcal{B} \in \mathbb{B}} \mathcal{B}) = \sum_{\mathcal{B} \in \mathbb{B}} P_{total}(\mathcal{B}) = \sum_{\mathcal{B} \in \mathbb{B}} 0 = 0
 
Second, for the set \mathcal{C} of non-self-consistent chaotic states, its elements do not satisfy any stable rules or causal constraints, and cannot form a measurable set with stable boundaries, so its global outer measure is naturally zero, that is, P_{total}(\mathcal{C}) = 0.
 
In summary, P_{total}(\mathcal{U}) = P_{total}(\mathcal{S} \cup \mathcal{C}) = P_{total}(\mathcal{S}) + P_{total}(\mathcal{C}) = 0 + 0 = 0.
 
Path 2: Proof of Creation ex nihilo with Measure Conservation
 
If we assume P_{total}(\mathcal{U}) > 0, we will inevitably face the origin dilemma of "where does the positive-measure total-state space come from", returning to the logical paradox of the first cause. The setting of P_{total}(\mathcal{U}) = 0 naturally meets the requirement of measure conservation: the total weight of the total-state space is zero, without any initial "energy injection" or "first cause". The emergence and evolution of self-consistent bubbles will never break the conservation of the global total measure of zero, which perfectly realizes the self-consistent explanation of creation ex nihilo of the universe origin.
 
Path 3: Proof from Closure Property of Null Sets
 
In the total-state space, all state sets that can be defined and described are null sets; while the undefinable and indescribable chaotic states also do not have the definability of positive measure. According to the monotonicity of measure, the measure of the whole space must be less than or equal to the supremum of the measures of all subsets, and the supremum of the measures of all subsets is zero, so P_{total}(\mathcal{U}) = 0.
 
Q.E.D.
 
3.2 Local Normalization Theorem of Self-Consistent Probability Bubbles
 
Theorem 2 (Local Normalization Theorem): For any self-consistent probability bubble \mathcal{B} satisfying Definition 3, its intrinsic probability measure P_\mathcal{B} must satisfy the normalization: P_\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{B}) = 1.
 
Proof: We complete the proof of this theorem from three dimensions: the mandatory constraint of probability axioms, the inevitable result of self-consistency and closure, and the natural locking of the observer's perspective.
 
Dimension 1: Mandatory Constraints of Probability Axioms
 
One of the core attributes of a self-consistent probability bubble \mathcal{B} is that a complete measurable space (\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{F}_\mathcal{B}) can be defined, and the event probability and evolution prediction inside the bubble need to be expanded based on the probability measure on this space. One of the core axioms of Kolmogorov's axiomatic system is the normalization of probability measure: the measure of the whole sample space must be 1.
 
If a measure does not satisfy P_\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{B}) = 1, it is not a legal probability measure, cannot define the relative probability of events inside the bubble, and cannot support stable state evolution and rule reproducibility, which directly violates the premise of "rule self-consistency" of the self-consistent probability bubble. Therefore, as long as \mathcal{B} is a self-consistent probability bubble, its intrinsic measure must be a probability measure satisfying normalization.
 
Dimension 2: Inevitable Result of Self-Consistency and Closure
 
The causal closure of the self-consistent probability bubble determines that all evolution and observations inside the bubble can only be completed within \mathcal{B}, without causal coupling with the states outside the bubble. This means that all probability judgments inside the bubble are essentially conditional probability P(\cdot | \mathcal{B}), that is, "the probability of an event occurring under the premise that the self-consistent bubble \mathcal{B} exists and its intrinsic rules hold".
 
According to the standard definition of conditional probability:
P(A | \mathcal{B}) = \frac{P_{total}(A \cap \mathcal{B})}{P_{total}(\mathcal{B})}
Although from the global perspective P_{total}(\mathcal{B}) = 0, the conditional probability inside the bubble is defined within \mathcal{B}, and its core is the relative weight of events. In order to make the relative weight calculable and reproducible, the conditional probability must be normalized, which naturally satisfies P(\mathcal{B} | \mathcal{B}) = 1.
 
If the intrinsic total measure of a "bubble" is not 1, there are only two possibilities: one is that its rules are not self-consistent, resulting in probability divergence and logical paradoxes; the other is that it does not satisfy causal closure and has coupling with other bubbles, which is essentially just a subset of a larger self-consistent bubble, not an independent self-consistent probability bubble.
 
Dimension 3: Perspective Locking of Observers Inside the Bubble
 
For observers inside the bubble, all their observation, experiment, and cognitive activities can only be carried out within \mathcal{B}, and cannot perceive the total-state space \mathcal{U} outside the bubble, nor the attribute of P_{total}(\mathcal{B}) = 0 of the global outer measure. All possibilities that the observer can access are limited within the scope of \mathcal{B}, so their probability cognition of the world must be a normalized probability system with \mathcal{B} as the whole sample space, which naturally satisfies P_\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{B}) = 1.
 
Q.E.D.
 
3.3 Core Corollaries
 
Based on the above two theorems, we can directly obtain two key corollaries to further improve the logical closed loop of the model.
 
Corollary 1 (Existence of Null Measure Bubbles): All self-consistent probability bubbles \mathcal{B} are non-empty null sets in the total-state universe space \mathcal{U}, that is, P_{total}(\mathcal{B}) = 0 and \mathcal{B} \neq \emptyset.
 
This corollary directly explains the classic contradiction of "zero probability but possible occurrence": the self-consistent bubble has a measure of 0 from the global perspective, but it itself is a non-empty set containing achievable states, and the events inside it are completely possible to occur, which is essentially different from the impossible event corresponding to the empty set.
 
Corollary 2 (Non-Impossibility of Null Measure Events Within the Bubble): For any null measure event A inside the self-consistent probability bubble \mathcal{B} (i.e., P_\mathcal{B}(A) = 0), A is not an impossible event.
 
This corollary is a natural extension of the properties of null sets inside the bubble. For example, thermodynamic entropy decrease events, macroscopic quantum tunneling events, etc., have a probability of 0 under the intrinsic measure inside the bubble, but are not absolutely impossible to occur, only "almost certainly not to occur".
 
4. Physical and Philosophical Interpretation of the Model
 
The above mathematical framework is not a purely formal logical deduction, but has clear physical connotations and philosophical significance, and can give a new interpretation of the core problems of cosmology and quantum mechanics.
 
4.1 Interpretation of Creation ex nihilo of Cosmic Origin
 
Traditional cosmology has always been unable to avoid the problem of "where the universe comes from". Any model that presupposes a positive-probability initial state must explain the origin of the initial state, and eventually falls into the logical dilemma of infinite recursion.
 
This model completely solves this problem: the global total measure of the total-state universe is zero, which means that the entire possibility space does not need any initial "first cause", does not need to preset any positive-probability initial conditions, and does not need the pre-existence of energy or matter. The formation of a self-consistent probability bubble is essentially the natural emergence of a subset satisfying self-consistency constraints in the global zero-measure space, and its global measure is always zero, which will not break the global measure conservation, and truly realizes a self-consistent explanation of the origin of the universe with creation ex nihilo.
 
4.2 Locality and Relativity of Physical Laws
 
Traditional physics defaults to the existence of "unified physical laws of the whole universe", but it has never been able to explain "why the physical constants of our universe are these values" and "whether the physical laws remain unchanged everywhere and at all times in the universe".
 
This model gives a new interpretation: physical laws are not globally unified, but intrinsic attributes of self-consistent probability bubbles. Different self-consistent probability bubbles can have completely different physical constants, spacetime dimensions, causal rules, and even logical systems. These rules are only valid inside the bubble, and are only determined by the self-consistency constraints of the bubble. From a global perspective, there is no "only correct physical law", only countless locally self-consistent rule systems.
 
4.3 Observer's Perspective Locking and Relativity of Reality
 
The model perfectly explains the problem of "why the universe we perceive is real and deterministic": observers inside the bubble are naturally locked in the intrinsic perspective of the self-consistent bubble, and all their cognition and observation are based on the normalized probability measure inside the bubble. For the observer, the existence of the bubble they are in is deterministic, with an intrinsic probability of 1, so the perceived world is real, stable, and predictable.
 
At the same time, the model also clarifies the relativity of reality: different self-consistent probability bubbles correspond to different "realities", and observers inside each bubble will think that the world they are in is the only real one, while the global measure of other bubbles is zero and cannot be directly observed.
 
5. Resolution of Classic Paradoxes
 
Through the hierarchical division of measures, this model can directly resolve many classic paradoxes that have plagued cosmology, quantum mechanics, and probability philosophy for decades.
 
5.1 Resolution of the Probability Paradox of the Anthropic Principle
 
The core dilemma of the traditional anthropic principle is: the fundamental physical constants of our universe must meet extremely harsh conditions to give birth to stars, planets, and intelligent life. The probability of such a habitable universe appearing is extremely low, and the problem of "why such a small-probability event happens to occur" has always lacked a convincing explanation.
 
In this model, this paradox is completely resolved:
 
1. The measure of all self-consistent probability bubbles from the global perspective is zero, and there is no essential difference in the global weight between "habitable universes" and "non-habitable universes", and there is no such thing as "small probability".
2. Intelligent life can only be born in a self-consistent bubble that meets habitable conditions, and observers inside the bubble will naturally perceive that the intrinsic probability of the bubble they are in is 1. Our existence itself presupposes that we are in a self-consistent bubble that meets habitable conditions, and there is no need to additionally explain "why a small-probability event occurs".
 
5.2 Resolution of the Measure Conundrum of the Quantum Many-Worlds Interpretation
 
Everett's many-worlds interpretation is one of the mainstream interpretations of quantum mechanics, but it has always faced two core dilemmas: one is how to define and normalize the probability weights of countless world branches; the other is why the event probability perceived by observers conforms to the Born rule of quantum mechanics.
 
This model provides a perfect mathematical framework for the many-worlds interpretation:
 
1. Every world branch generated by quantum decoherence is a sub self-consistent bubble split from the original self-consistent bubble. The measure of each sub-bubble from the global perspective is zero, and there is no problem of "total probability divergence caused by infinite branches".
2. Inside each sub-bubble, there is an intrinsic probability measure satisfying normalization, and observers inside the bubble will perceive that the branch they are in is deterministic with a probability of 1.
3. The intrinsic probability measure of the original bubble can naturally assign weights to different sub-branches, which is perfectly compatible with the probability prediction of the Born rule, and solves the problem of probability weight in the many-worlds interpretation.
 
5.3 Resolution of the Intuitive Contradiction Between "Zero Probability and Possible Occurrence"
 
Since the birth of probability theory, the conclusion that "the probability of a single-point event in a continuous space is zero, but it is not an impossible event" has always been contrary to the public's intuition, and has also become a core controversy in probability philosophy.
 
Through the hierarchical division of measures, this model completely clarifies the essence of this contradiction:
 
1. "Zero probability" is a weight attribute under a specific measure system, not an attribute of "existence". From the global measure, the measure of the self-consistent bubble is zero, which does not mean it does not exist; from the intrinsic measure inside the bubble, the probability of a single-point event is zero, which does not mean it cannot occur.
2. Only the impossible event corresponding to the empty set is "absolutely never going to happen", and all non-empty null sets have the possibility of occurrence. There is a strict mathematical boundary between the two, and they cannot be equated.
 
5.4 Resolution of the Exception Problem of the Second Law of Thermodynamics
 
The entropy increase principle of the second law of thermodynamics is the core physical law of the macroscopic world, but its statistical mechanics essence determines that the "entropy decrease event" is not absolutely impossible, but the probability of occurrence is extremely low.
 
In this model, the entropy increase principle is a law that holds "almost everywhere" inside the self-consistent bubble, that is, the set of events that do not satisfy entropy increase is a null set inside the bubble. This means:
 
1. The entropy increase principle has absolute predictive validity in the macroscopic evolution inside the bubble, because the intrinsic probability of entropy decrease events is zero, and it is almost certain not to occur in a limited observation time and space scale.
2. Entropy decrease events are not absolutely impossible. They still belong to the achievable events inside the bubble, but are negligible in the sense of measure, which is perfectly compatible with the statistical essence of the second law of thermodynamics and the possibility of exceptions.
 
6. Discussion and Outlook
 
The "global zero measure - local normalization" self-consistent probability bubble universe model proposed in this paper has three core advantages:
 
1. Complete self-consistency: The model is completely based on standard ZFC set theory, measure theory and axiomatic probability theory, without introducing any additional, unfalsifiable physical hypotheses. All core conclusions have strict mathematical proofs, and the logic is closed and non-contradictory.
2. Strong explanatory power: The model can simultaneously resolve classic paradoxes in many fields such as cosmic origin, anthropic principle, quantum many-worlds, and probability philosophy, and provides a unified interpretation framework for core problems in different fields.
3. Minimal underlying logic: The core of the model is only to distinguish the hierarchical boundary between "global outer measure" and "local intrinsic measure", break the default assumption that "the whole space must be probability normalized", and solve complex underlying dilemmas with minimal logical conversion.
 
At the same time, the model also provides a number of expandable directions for follow-up research:
 
1. Research on strict criteria for self-consistency: The definition of self-consistent probability bubbles in this paper gives the core constraints, but has not yet formed a strict mathematical criterion for self-consistency. Follow-up research can further study the mathematical expression of "rule self-consistency and causal closure", and clarify the formation conditions and boundaries of self-consistent bubbles.
2. Research on the formation and evolution mechanism of bubbles: The model clarifies the mathematical attributes of self-consistent bubbles, but has not yet discussed the dynamic mechanism of the formation, evolution, splitting and merging of bubbles. Follow-up research can combine quantum field theory and cosmic inflation theory to study the dynamic evolution law of self-consistent bubbles.
3. Research on the correlation between intrinsic measure and physical rules: The model clarifies the binding relationship between the intrinsic measure inside the bubble and the physical rules. Follow-up research can further study how different self-consistent rule systems determine the corresponding intrinsic probability measure, and the internal correlation between this measure and the Born rule of quantum mechanics.
4. Research on the possibility of inter-bubble interactions: The model assumes that self-consistent bubbles have causal closure and no uncontrollable coupling with states outside the bubble, but follow-up research can study whether there are definable, weak interactions between different self-consistent bubbles, and whether such interactions will produce observable physical effects.
