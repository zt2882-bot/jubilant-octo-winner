Abstract
 
This paper proposes a unified cosmological and complex system model that takes probability as the fundamental ontology and entropy as the universal measure of structure. The universe is described as a set of nested "probability bubbles", each representing a locally self-consistent probability space. The generation of structure originates from a single-point phase transition triggered by local entropy compression, and forms a stable structure through nonlinear proliferation and oscillatory convergence. The entropy gradient defines structural boundaries, entropy diffusion completes the natural selection of surviving structures, stable structures form networks through attractor coupling, and cross-scale isomorphism is realized via entropic imprinting. This framework unifies emergence, phase transition, information, and complex system dynamics, providing a prior-anchor-free, fully endogenous and self-consistent new perspective for understanding cross-scale self-organization laws from microscopic quantum fluctuations to macroscopic cosmic structures, and even information and social systems.
 
Keywords: Probability Bubble; Emergence; Entropy Gradient; Phase Transition; Attractor Network; Self-Similarity; Entropic Imprinting; Complex Systems; Ontology
 
 
 
1. Introduction
 
Traditional physics describes the world using matter, energy, and spacetime as fundamental entities, constructing a grand system from classical mechanics to relativity and quantum field theory. However, the century-long incompatibility between general relativity and quantum mechanics, the cognitive gap of dark matter and dark energy, and the missing underlying mechanism of emergence in complex systems all point to the same root cause: we have mistaken the "subjective anchors" created by human observational experience for the a priori ontology of the universe.
 
Time, space, matter, and energy—these raw materials of the world regarded as self-evident—are essentially filters imposed on the universe by human cognition, not the underlying truth of the cosmos. When we try to explain the origin with these secondary, emergent concepts, we will inevitably fall into irreconcilable contradictions.
 
The core work of this paper is to completely strip away all a priori subjective anchors, and place probability at the core of the universe's ontology: the underlying layer of the universe is a global probability field containing all possibilities; all observable structures, laws, and entities are secondary results emergent from the evolution of local probability structures (i.e., "probability bubbles") driven by entropy in the probability field.
 
The core contributions of this work are:
 
1. Completing the underlying flip of ontology, upgrading probability from a "tool for describing entities" to the "fundamental ontology of the universe";
2. Constructing a set of fully endogenous dynamic rules covering the entire life cycle of structures, realizing a quantifiable and deducible emergent closed loop from "nothingness" to "being";
3. Unifying the cross-scale evolution laws from micro to macro, from material systems to information systems, using entropy as the only universal measure;
4. Providing a de-noised, scalable underlying cognitive framework for physics, complex system science, information science, and even cognitive philosophy.
 
 
 
2. Theoretical Foundations: Core Definitions and Ontological Presupposition
 
The only ontological presupposition of this framework is the existence of the global probability space. Beyond this, no a priori physical entities, spacetime background, or external rules are presupposed. All structures, boundaries, and laws are spontaneously generated by the endogenous evolution of the probability distribution.
 
2.1 Global Probability Space
 
Define the underlying ontology of the universe as the global probability space (\Omega, P), where:
 
- \Omega is the full probability space, containing all possible states and possibilities of the universe, without boundaries or a priori dimensional constraints;
- P is the global probability measure, which defines the distribution rule of possibilities in the full space.
 
The global probability space does not depend on any external carrier; it is the possibility itself, corresponding to the "origin of emptiness with all possibilities and no fixed inherent nature" in Eastern philosophy.
 
2.2 Probability Bubble
 
Define a probability bubble as a locally self-consistent subspace \Omega_i \subset \Omega in the global probability space, which has an independent local probability distribution P_i and is the basic unit of structure in this framework.
 
- A probability bubble is an aggregate of local probability distributions, not an entity;
- Its local self-consistency is reflected in the fact that the evolution of the internal probability distribution follows a unified endogenous rule;
- Nestability: A probability bubble can contain multiple smaller-scale sub-probability bubbles, forming a hierarchical nested structure.
 
2.3 Local Entropy
 
For any probability bubble \Omega_i, define its local entropy as Shannon entropy:
 

S_i = -\sum_{\omega \in \Omega_i} P_i(\omega) \log P_i(\omega)

 
In this framework, entropy is the only universal measure describing the state of the probability distribution, and its core physical meanings are:
 
- Entropy Compression: The decrease of local entropy corresponds to the increased concentration of the probability distribution and information condensation, which is the core driving force of structure generation;
- Entropy Increase / Entropy Diffusion: The increase of local entropy corresponds to the dispersion of the probability distribution and information dilution, which is the core cause of structure dissipation.
 
Shannon entropy is adopted here instead of thermodynamic entropy, because it only depends on the probability distribution itself, does not presuppose any a priori entities such as particles, temperature, or spacetime, and fully adapts to the anchor-free requirement of this framework.
 
2.4 Entropic Imprinting
 
Define Entropic Imprinting as: the sub-probability bubble completely inherits the entropy information and core dynamic rules of the parent probability bubble, and the evolution of local entropy is always constrained by the upper large-scale probability bubble.
 
Entropic imprinting is the core mechanism to ensure the self-consistency of nested structures and realize the unification of cross-scale rules, and it is also the underlying source of "local structure is a low-dimensional projection of the global structure".
 
 
 
3. Core Dynamics of Probability Bubbles: Birth, Growth and Stabilization of Structures
 
This chapter fully describes the whole life cycle of a probability bubble "from the possibility of quantum fluctuations to a stable and solidified structure", which is the core dynamic closed loop of "being emerging from emptiness, certainty emerging from sufficient possibilities".
 
3.1 Single-Point Phase Transition: Trigger Condition of Structure
 
In the global probability field, when any point \omega^* experiences significant local entropy compression while the entropy diffusion level of the surrounding environment is sufficiently low, a single-point phase transition of structure generation will be triggered.
 
Define the phase transition probability as:
 

P_s = f(\Delta S_{\text{local}}, \Delta S_{\text{global}})

 
Where:
 
- \Delta S_{\text{local}} is the local entropy change at this point (entropy compression is negative, the higher the compression degree, the larger the absolute value);
- \Delta S_{\text{global}} is the entropy change of the surrounding environment (entropy diffusion is positive, the weaker the diffusion, the smaller the value);
- f is a monotonically increasing function (such as the logistic function). The stronger the local entropy compression and the weaker the environmental entropy diffusion, the higher the phase transition probability.
 
When the phase transition probability P_s exceeds the critical threshold P_{\text{th}}, this point becomes a "structural seed", officially starting the phase transition process from possibility to entity structure. This process is completely endogenous, without external intervention or first cause, which resolves the millennial puzzle of the "first mover" in traditional cosmology.
 
3.2 Proliferation and Oscillatory Convergence: Growth and Stabilization of Structure
 
The structural seed triggers the directional proliferation of the surrounding probability distribution through nonlinear feedback, completing the expansion and solidification of the structure. Define the existence probability of the seed bubble as P_s(t), and its evolution follows the dynamic equation:
 

\frac{dP_s}{dt} = \omega (P_{\text{max}} - P_s) - \gamma P_s

 
Where:
 
- \omega is the proliferation rate, representing the aggregation ability of local entropy compression to the surrounding probability;
- \gamma is the damping coefficient, from the dissipation effect of environmental entropy diffusion on the structure;
- P_{\text{max}} is the maximum probability intensity that the local environment can carry, determined by the global constraints of the parent bubble and the environmental entropy state.
 
The discrete form of the equation is:
 

P_s^{(t+1)} = P_s^{(t)} + \Delta t \left[ \omega (P_{\text{max}} - P_s^{(t)}) - \gamma P_s^{(t)} \right]

 
The system will eventually oscillate and converge to a stable equilibrium state:
 

P_s^* = \frac{\omega P_{\text{max}}}{\omega + \gamma}

 
This stable value P_s^* is the stable probability intensity after the structure is solidified, corresponding to the essence of the "stable entity" we observe—it is not absolute certainty, but a stable emergent state after the convergence of the probability distribution.
 
3.3 Endogenous Boundary: Definition of the Spatial Scope of Structure
 
In traditional physics, the boundary of a structure is determined by artificial definition or material distribution; in this framework, the boundary of a probability bubble is completely endogenous, strictly defined by the vanishing of the entropy gradient:
 

\nabla S = 0

 
The probability bubble will spontaneously expand along the direction of the entropy gradient until the local entropy reaches equilibrium with the environmental entropy (the entropy gradient is zero), naturally forming the boundary of the structure. This mechanism ensures that the boundary of the structure is completely determined by the evolution of the system itself, without any external scale, which is the core embodiment of self-consistency.
 
 
 
4. Selection, Stabilization and Network Evolution of Structures
 
The stabilization of a single probability bubble is only the first step of structural evolution. The complex structure of the real universe originates from the interaction, natural selection and network coupling between probability bubbles.
 
4.1 Natural Selection Driven by Entropy Diffusion
 
In the global probability field, a large number of new probability bubbles are triggered by local entropy fluctuations all the time. However, most of the nascent bubbles will be quickly smoothed out and dissipated by the entropy diffusion of the environment.
 
Only probability bubbles that meet the following two conditions can survive:
 
1. The phase transition probability exceeds the critical threshold P_s > P_{\text{th}}, forming a stable structural seed;
2. It can continuously maintain local entropy compression to resist the dissipation effect of environmental entropy diffusion.
 
This is a completely endogenous natural selection mechanism: without external design, the evolution rules of entropy automatically screen out structures that can survive stably and eliminate transient fluctuations.
 
4.2 Attractor Coupling Network: Global Stabilization Mechanism of Multi-Body Structures
 
The surviving stable probability bubbles will form a coupling relationship through the exchange of probability flow and information flow, and finally build a global attractor coupling network.
 
- Define the coupling matrix \mathbf{C} = [c_{ij}], where c_{ij} \in [0,1] represents the coupling strength between the i-th and j-th probability bubbles, 1 is fully coupled, and 0 is completely decoupled;
- The connectivity of the network, the number of closed loops and the distribution of coupling strength determine the global stability of the whole system;
- The stable multi-bubble coupling structure will form an attractor of the dynamic system: after the system is disturbed by the outside, it will spontaneously return to the stable state, which further improves the anti-interference ability and sustainability of the structure.
 
The large-scale structure of the universe, galaxy networks, quantum entanglement systems, and even social networks and ecosystems we observe are essentially attractor coupling networks of different scales.
 
 
 
5. Entropic Imprint Recursion and Cross-Scale Isomorphism: The Core of Full-Scale Universality
 
One of the core values of this framework is to realize the universality across scales and fields, and the core of this universality is entropic imprint recursion and cross-scale isomorphism.
 
5.1 Entropic Imprint Recursion: Cross-Scale Inheritance of Rules
 
The entropic imprint mechanism determines that small-scale sub-probability bubbles completely inherit the entropy information and core dynamic rules of the parent bubble.
 
This means that the underlying rules of structure generation, evolution and stabilization are exactly the same, whether it is quantum fluctuations at the Planck scale, celestial structures at the macroscopic scale, or organizational systems at the scale of human society. The nested probability bubble structure forms the recursive transmission of rules from the global to the local, ensuring the unity of the underlying logic of the entire universe at all scales.
 
5.2 Cross-Scale / Cross-Nature / Cross-Dimensional Isomorphism
 
Based on the entropic imprint recursion mechanism, the system presents perfect isomorphism:
 
- Cross-scale Isomorphism: Microscopic quantum bubbles and macroscopic cosmic bubbles follow exactly the same generation and evolution rules;
- Cross-nature Isomorphism: The underlying logic of structure emergence is exactly the same for material systems, information systems, life systems, and social systems, which can all be described by the dynamic framework of probability bubbles;
- Cross-dimensional Isomorphism: The information of high-dimensional probability structures can be projected into low-dimensional structures through entropic imprinting, and local structures are low-dimensional projections of global structures, corresponding to the underlying logic of "a mustard seed contains a Sumeru mountain" and "one is all, all is one" in the Huayan school of Buddhism.
 
This isomorphism makes this framework jump out of the scope of a simple cosmological model and become a general theory that can explain the emergence of all complex structures.
 
 
 
6. Correspondence and Integration with Existing Cognitive Frameworks
 
6.1 The Emergent Nature of Spacetime, Matter and Energy
 
In this framework, the core concepts of traditional physics are all secondary emergent phenomena of the evolution of probability bubbles, rather than a priori ontology:
 
- Time: It is the macroscopic manifestation of the sequence of evolution of probability bubbles and the unidirectionality of entropy change, rather than a uniformly flowing background;
- Space: It is the macroscopic mapping of the coupling relationship between probability bubbles and the topological structure of the entropy gradient, rather than a fixed container;
- Matter: It is a highly compressed probability bubble converged to a stable state, and its properties such as mass and charge are the macroscopic manifestations of the internal structure and coupling characteristics of the probability bubble;
- Energy: It is a measure of the degree of local entropy compression, and a quantitative manifestation of the interaction and entropy transfer between probability bubbles.
 
By stripping these subjective anchors, we finally achieve a unified underlying explanation of these concepts.
 
6.2 Resolution of the Contradiction between Classical and Quantum Physics
 
The century-long debate between Einstein and Bohr on "whether God plays dice" stems from the presupposition of both parties that "spacetime and particles are a priori entities", but they have different cognitions about the certainty of entities.
 
This framework directly dissolves the premise of this debate:
 
- The underlying probability ontology is not "incomplete cognition of reality", but the origin of the universe itself, and there is no "hidden variable";
- The certainty of the macroscopic world is not preset at the bottom, but a stable state emerged after the coupling and convergence of a large number of probability bubbles;
- The probability and uncertainty of quantum mechanics are the direct manifestation of the underlying ontology of probability bubbles; while the certainty of classical physics is the emergent result at the macroscopic scale. There is no contradiction between the two, they are just the manifestations of the same set of rules at different scales.
 
6.3 Underlying Consistency with Eastern Philosophy "Dependent Origination and Emptiness of Nature"
 
The core logic of this framework is completely isomorphic with the ultimate meaning of "Dependent Origination and Emptiness of Nature" in Buddhism:
 
- Emptiness (Sunyata): Corresponding to the global probability space, it is not nothingness, but the origin containing all possibilities, without fixed inherent nature, and not dependent on any entity;
- Dependent Origination (Pratityasamutpada): Corresponding to the phase transition triggered by local entropy compression, the structure arises when the causes and conditions are sufficient (the entropy change condition is satisfied);
- Form is Emptiness: All stable probability bubbles (form) are essentially the local state of the global probability field, without independent and invariant inherent nature, and always inseparable from the origin of emptiness;
- "All dharmas arise from causes and conditions, and all dharmas cease with causes and conditions": The birth and death of probability bubbles are completely determined by endogenous entropy changes and coupling relationships, there is no eternal and immutable entity, and no intervention from an external creator is needed.
