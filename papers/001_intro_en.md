# Gap Library · About This Project

This is not an open-source project. There is no centralized roadmap. No one is responsible for answering questions.

This is a collection of **independent papers** that share a single underlying structure — constraint signature matching — even though each paper appears to come from a completely different discipline.

## Why GitHub Issues as a Publishing Medium

Because this is not academic publication. Not seeking citations. Not waiting for peer review. These are signposts — for those who have already reached the same structures within their own domains, but don't know that others are looking at the same thing.

## How to Read

1. Find the paper closest to you. Browse by discipline label — whether you work in cryptography, cosmology, quantum information, or cognitive science, there is one written from your domain.
2. Read the cross-references in the footnotes. Each paper points to others that share the same constraint signature. If your paper references cryptography AND linguistics — it is because those two domains project the same constraint structure onto your problem.
3. If you discover that your own work shares this structure — and you have data or code to validate it — proceed to the third layer.

## Three Layers

### Layer 1 · Cross-Validation
You have independently verified the existence of constraint signatures within your domain. You read a paper here and discover that it describes the same structure in another domain. You do not need to be convinced — you need to say "me too." This layer has no public entry point. Contact only through known constraint signature mapping.

### Layer 2 · Co-Building
You have read the papers here, understood the underlying structure, and possess engineering capability. This layer has concrete project tasks. No academic background required. What's required is the intuition to "see constraint structures."

### Layer 3 · Information Relay
You have seen valuable papers, datasets, or contacts in your domain — resources likely valuable for cross-reference to other domains here. Send them over. No participation required. No commitment required. Information you relay will be cross-validated — if it triggers a constraint signature collision, you will receive confirmation.
