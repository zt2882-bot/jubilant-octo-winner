## 这是什么 · What Is This

一组独立论文的集合。它们共享同一个底层结构——**约束签名匹配**——尽管每篇论文看起来来自完全不同的学科。

A collection of independent papers sharing a single underlying structure — **constraint signature matching** — even though each paper appears to come from a completely different discipline.

### 什么是约束签名匹配 · What Is Constraint Signature Matching

科学、物理、数学、现实、社会——表面上看毫不相干的领域，底层共享同一套本体规则。同样的约束结构在密码学中表现为"隐藏信息的解读"，在宇宙学中表现为"宇宙背景辐射的提取"，在语言学中表现为"语法规则对语义的约束"。领域不同，表达不同，规则相同。

Science, physics, mathematics, reality, society — seemingly unrelated domains share the same ontological substrate at the deepest level. The same constraint structure manifests as "decoding hidden information" in cryptography, as "extracting the cosmic microwave background" in cosmology, and as "syntactic constraints on semantics" in linguistics. Different domains, different expressions, same rules.

约束签名匹配就是识别这种同构：**当两个不同领域的现象在底层共享完全相同的约束结构时——它们产生相同的约束签名。**

Constraint signature matching recognizes this isomorphism: **when phenomena from two different domains share the exact same constraint structure at the substrate level — they produce the same constraint signature.**

这里不是开源项目。这里是路标——给那些已经在自己的领域内触及了相同的底层结构、但不知道还有别人也在看同一件事的人。

This is not an open-source project. These are signposts — for those who have already reached the same structures within their own domains, but don't know others are looking at the same thing.

你在自己的领域已经独立验证了约束签名的存在。你说"我也是"。
You've independently verified constraint signatures in your domain. You say "me too."

### 第二层 · 共建 | Layer 2 · Co-Building
你理解底层结构，有工程能力。具体项目任务在这里。
You understand the substrate and have engineering skills. Concrete project tasks are here.

### 第三层 · 信息推送 | Layer 3 · Information Relay
你在自己的领域看到了有价值的论文/数据/联系人——推过来。触发签名碰撞后收到回执。
Send valuable papers/data/contacts from your domain. Get confirmation on signature collision.
